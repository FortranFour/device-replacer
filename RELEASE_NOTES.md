# Device Replacer 1.2.3 — prerelease

The successful transfer message now gives the complete next step: open **Settings → Devices & services → Entities**, open the replacement sensor's settings, and turn on **Enabled**. It explicitly reminds you to leave the old sensor disabled. The same directions appear when **Check outcome** verifies a transfer that completed late, and in the README.

This is a wording update. Transfer and entity-disable behavior are unchanged. Re-enabling the replacement remains a manual action in its Home Assistant entity settings. This remains a testing prerelease; v1.1.3 is the latest stable release.
