# Device Replacer 1.2.2 — prerelease

Clarify the statistics section's setup directions: choose **Single entity**, search for or enter the old sensor in **Entity to replace** and the new sensor in **Replacement entity**, then click **Check statistics**. The instructions also explain the **Whole device** mapping alternative.

This is a wording update. Statistics transfer and the sensor-disable controls are unchanged from v1.2.1. The measurement-only restrictions remain, including rejection of existing replacement statistics and cumulative counters. This remains a testing prerelease; v1.1.3 is the latest stable release.
